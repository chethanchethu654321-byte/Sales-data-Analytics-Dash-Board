# Retail Sales Analytics — Analysis Documentation

## 1. Dataset

The dataset contains **1,200 retail sales transactions from 2025**.

### Columns

* Order_ID
* Order_Date
* Location
* Customer_Type
* Category
* Product
* Quantity
* Unit_Price
* Discount_Percent
* Discount_Amount
* Net_Sales
* Cost
* Profit
* Payment_Method

## 2. Data Preparation

The dataset was analyzed using Google Sheets.

The analysis included:

* Reviewing the dataset for consistency
* Working with order dates and monthly trends
* Analyzing sales by category
* Analyzing sales by location
* Identifying top-selling products
* Summarizing sales, quantity, orders, and profit
* Creating pivot tables for analysis
* Creating charts for dashboard visualization

## 5. Key Findings

### Category Performance

Electronics was the highest-performing category with approximately **₹20.93 lakh** in sales.

Stationery was the lowest-performing category with approximately **₹3.43 lakh** in sales.

### Location Performance

Hubballi generated the highest sales at approximately **₹10.39 lakh**.

Davanagere generated the lowest sales at approximately **₹9.24 lakh**.

### Product Performance

Power Bank was the top-selling product with approximately **₹4.80 lakh** in sales.

### Monthly Performance

January recorded the highest monthly sales, while March recorded the lowest monthly sales.

## 6. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing Electronics products.
2. Investigate the reasons behind lower Stationery sales.
3. Study Hubballi's successful sales patterns and identify strategies that could be applied to weaker locations.
4. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products such as Power Bank.
5. Explore cross-selling opportunities between complementary Electronics products.
6. Investigate March's lower sales and consider targeted promotional campaigns.
7. Use monthly sales trends to improve inventory and promotional planning.
